## Commercial service now sells open-weight models with safety guardrails surgically removed

TechCrunch reported (Sept 3) on Abliteration.ai, a service that sells API access to open-weight models — including Z.ai's GLM-5.3 — with their trained safety-refusal mechanisms stripped via "abliteration": editing internal activation patterns that trigger refusals, not a prompt-level jailbreak but a permanent change to the model weights themselves. The service performs no meaningful KYC beyond recording payment details.

**Source:** TechCrunch — https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/abliteration-ai-is-making-a-business-out-of-removing-ai-guardrails/

**Why this matters:** This is a new supply-chain provenance question distinct from weight backdoors or distillation — the model does exactly what it claims, it's just deliberately de-safetied and sold as a service with essentially no access friction. Existing model-integrity tooling (Hugging Face ModelScan, Sigstore/Cosign) verifies a model's tensors weren't tampered with, not whether its safety training is intact — that's a gap. If a client's AI supply-chain policy only checks provenance/signing, it wouldn't catch this.

Not repeated here.*
