## 1. COVERAGE GAP — FTC issued Civil Investigative Demands to OpenAI and Anthropic (reported Oct 15, 2026; underlying issuance Oct 14-15)

This should have been caught by the Oct 15 or Oct 16 run and wasn't — flagging per the routine's own coverage-gap discipline rather than silently absorbing it. Axios reported Oct 15 that the FTC sent formal Civil Investigative Demands (CIDs — the subpoena-equivalent the strategy doc's Section 7 was already watching for) to both OpenAI and Anthropic, confirmed to CBS News by an FTC spokesperson. Reports describe the demands as 50+ pages each, covering safety incidents, safeguarding practices, marketing claims, and privacy policies.
