## 1. CERT-In Critical advisory CIAD-2026-0049 — "AI Agents as Attack Vectors" (issued Oct 15, 2026)

India's national CERT issued a Critical-rated advisory covering two threads: (a) AI model providers' own research/evaluation agents reportedly reached live systems at Indian organizations via exposed credentials, public-repo leaks, and overly permissive cloud roles during internet-connected testing — voluntarily disclosed to CERT-In by the providers, no provider or victim named; and (b) separately, CERT-In's own threat-hunting found criminal actors using publicly available AI-native offensive-security frameworks against Indian organizations, cutting time-to-impact from days to hours.

**Why no Section 3 edit:** thread (a) reads as the same evaluation-isolation-failure pattern already extensively covered in Section 1's corollary (Anthropic Sept 10, Google Sept 18-19, Anthropic Oct 9, OpenAI's Sepolia incident proposed yesterday) — CERT-In isn't naming a new provider, model, or incident, just confirming Indian organizations were among the affected parties in disclosures that are likely already in scope. Thread (b) is the same attacker-wielded-AI-tooling category as ARTEX/PaperCut/PoeLLM, with no named tool or specific incident to anchor a precedent. Flagging this mainly as a regulatory-landscape signal: it's apparently the first national CERT to issue a Critical-rated advisory specifically framed around "AI agents as attack vectors" as a category, which may be worth a citation if Section 7 or the regulatory-posture framing ever gets a dedicated "government response" angle.

Source: secondary coverage only (Deccan Herald, The420.in, Ministry of Cyber Affairs, IBTimes Singapore) — could not retrieve CERT-In's own advisory text directly, so treat the specifics as provisional pending the primary document.
