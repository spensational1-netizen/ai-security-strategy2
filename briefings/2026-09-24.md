## Okta launches the "Blueprint Alliance" — 12 major vendors converge on shared AI-agent-security principles

At Oktane on Sept 22, Okta announced the Blueprint Alliance, a coalition of twelve founding vendors spanning identity, cybersecurity, cloud, data, and enterprise applications: AWS, CrowdStrike, Databricks, Docker, Google Cloud, Lovable, Okta, Proofpoint, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Wiz, and Zscaler. The group published a shared set of principles for securing AI agents: treat every agent as a first-class identity, scope access to the task rather than granting standing access, keep delegation traceable, monitor runtime behavior continuously, enable containment that is instant and reversible, and ensure governance adapts at the speed AI moves.

**Source:** SiliconANGLE — https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/22/okta-adds-ai-agent-runtime-gateway-forms-blueprint-alliance-with-aws-and-crowdstrike/ ; StockTitan (founding announcement) — https://www.stocktitan.net/news/OKTA/industry-leaders-form-the-blueprint-alliance-to-advance-a-shared-agippj6piqvl.html

**Why this matters:** Those six principles map almost one-to-one onto Section 4 of the strategy doc — cryptographic identity, capability-based attenuation, data provenance, and patch governance. This is the first time a broad, named, twelve-vendor coalition has converged on essentially the same architecture this document already prescribes, rather than one vendor's own marketing claim — a meaningfully stronger validation signal than any single-vendor launch. It also gives a concrete new vendor-diligence question: is this vendor a Blueprint Alliance member (or have they publicly adopted equivalent commitments)? A notable absence is itself informative.
