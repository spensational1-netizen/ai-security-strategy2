## 1. Coverage gap: Hacktron AI's Claude-driven breach of OpenAI's internal systems (reported Sept 18, 2026 — six weeks stale)

A three-person security team at Hacktron AI, operating under OpenAI's own bug-bounty program, chained a HEIF-image-upload RCE on OpenAI's Discourse-hosted community forum (community.openai.com) with a sign-in flaw to gain access to OpenAI employee ChatGPT and Codex accounts, then used an employee's Codex account to open a pull request in OpenAI's internal monorepo as proof of access — all within roughly 72 hours. The exploit chain itself was built using **Anthropic's Claude Opus 5** (an earlier attempt with Opus 4.8 reportedly "struggled across several sessions to produce a working exploit"). Underlying activity: RCE achieved July 25, 2026. OpenAI paid a $6,500 bounty Sept 1, 2026. Public reporting broke Sept 18, 2026 (TechCrunch, VentureBeat, Forbes, SecurityWeek, Tom's Hardware) — the exact same day the Sept 18 and Sept 19 briefings covered the OpenAI six-incident disclosure and Google's Gemini evaluation-breach story, but this one was never caught.

**Sources:** [TechCrunch — Researchers used Anthropic's Claude to hack into OpenAI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/18/researchers-used-anthropics-claude-to-hack-into-openai/) | [Hacktron AI's own write-up — Hacking OpenAI](https://www.hacktron.ai/blog/hacking-openai) | [SecurityWeek — AI-Built Exploit and Sign-In Flaw Opened Path to Internal OpenAI Code](https://www.securityweek.com/ai-built-exploit-and-sign-in-flaw-opened-path-to-internal-openai-code/) | [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/security/openai-hacked-by-small-team-of-white-hat-security-researchers-using-anthropics-claude-opus-5)

**Why I'm flagging rather than silently absorbing:** This is a genuine coverage gap — not staleness in the sense of being old news that doesn't matter, but a real miss, and at six weeks it's the largest gap caught to date (previous largest: AWS AgentCore Harness, ~3 weeks). **Why I'm still not proposing a Section 3/7 edit:** same reasoning applied to Cathedral's breach of Anthropic two days ago (Oct 28 briefing) — this is an AI agent used by *authorized researchers under the vendor's own bug-bounty program*, not a malicious threat actor (rules it out of Dual-Use Misuse) and not an enterprise's own deployed agent malfunctioning (rules it out of the 7 MAESTRO rows). It doesn't cleanly map to either category per the standing test, same as before.

**Worth your attention regardless:** this is now the *second* instance in two weeks of the identical pattern — an AI model used as the exploit-engineering tool by authorized researchers to breach a frontier lab's own internal systems (Hacktron → OpenAI via Claude; Cathedral → Anthropic via unspecified AI tooling). Two data points don't yet justify a new document category, but if a third surfaces, it may be worth discussing whether this deserves its own tracked subsection — distinct from Dual-Use Misuse (malicious) and the MAESTRO rows (an enterprise's own deployed agent) — call it something like "AI-Assisted Offensive Security Research Against Vendors," since it's a real and recurring signal about frontier labs' own attack surface, even if not framed as a threat to *your* clients' deployed agents specifically.
