## 1. Trump announces "Super Intelligence Force" federal AI task force — new Section 7 item

President Trump announced (Oct 3–5) a federal task force led by DNI Jay Clayton, with FTC Chairman Andrew Ferguson, Under Secretary of Defense Emil Michael, and OPM Director Scott Kupor also serving. It's directed to assess AI risks/opportunities within 120 days, handle federal engagement with AI developers and critical infrastructure providers, develop AI-threat response plans, audit government alert systems, and support private-sector risk management. Reports to Trump and Chief of Staff Susie Wiles.

- Source: [Federal News Network](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/10/trump-names-national-intelligence-director-jay-clayton-to-lead-a-new-federal-ai-task-force/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/03/trump-jay-clayton-ai-czar.html), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/10/04/nx-s1-5990781/jay-clayton-ai-czar-trump)
- **Why this matters:** A new, broader federal coordination structure alongside the FTC's existing rogue-agent investigation — notably, the sitting FTC Chairman sits on the task force too, linking the two administratively. Proposed as a new Section 7 subsection.

## 2. Coverage-gap flag: Microsoft SQL Copilot prompt-injection-to-sysadmin (CVE-2026-65669) — Spencer's call on backdating (4th item in the backlog)

Published Sept 8, 2026 (CVSS 9.6), missed at the time. SQL Copilot in SQL Server Management Studio 22 enforced read-only mode via a bypassable regex blocklist — instructions planted in database extended properties (a field even a low-privilege attacker could poison) talked the Copilot agent out of read-only mode, ran arbitrary SQL as the victim user, and escalated to sysadmin. Fixed in SSMS 22.8.2, six weeks before the public advisory.

- Source: [Microsoft Security Response Center](https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/en-US/advisory/CVE-2026-65669), [Embrace The Red](https://embracethered.com/blog/posts/2026/from-select-to-sysadmin-sql-copilot-bluehat-asia/)
- **Why this matters:** Same mechanism class as SalesBleed (poisoned data field → unintended privileged action under the agent's trusted identity), against a different brownfield surface (database tooling), escalating all the way to sysadmin. Doesn't change Row 2's two-most-recent-inline items if approved — it's older than everything currently shown.
